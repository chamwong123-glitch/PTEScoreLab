# Summary: PTE Academic test format — Part 3, Listening

A summary in our own words of Pearson's official test-format page for Part 3:
<https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-academic/test-format/listening/> (read 19 September 2026).

Facts only. Pearson's text is not reproduced. For marking detail, [Score_Guide_Summary.md](Score_Guide_Summary.md) governs.

## The part at a glance

- **31–39 minutes**, 8 question types.
- **Each audio or video clip plays once, automatically.** Note-taking is allowed.
- **Chosen options are highlighted on screen.**

## Question types

| # | Question type | Audio | What you do | Skills | Scoring |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Summarize Spoken Text | 60–90 s | write a 50–70 word summary, with 10 minutes to listen and write; a word count is shown | Listening, Writing | Content, Form, Grammar, Vocabulary, Spelling. Partial credit; a human expert also reviews Content. Under 40 or over 100 words scores zero |
| 2 | Multiple Choice, Multiple Answers | 80–120 s | choose every correct option | Listening | partial credit* |
| 3 | Fill in the Blanks (Type In) | 30–60 s | type the missing word in each gap of a transcript; Tab moves between gaps | Listening | 1 point per word spelled correctly |
| 4 | Highlight Correct Summary | 30–90 s | choose the paragraph that best summarises the recording; only one is correct | Listening, Reading | correct or incorrect |
| 5 | Multiple Choice, Single Answer | 30–90 s | choose the one correct option | Listening | correct or incorrect |
| 6 | Select Missing Word | 20–70 s | the recording stops short; choose the ending from 3–5 options, only one correct. The next question waits for you to click Next | Listening | correct or incorrect |
| 7 | Highlight Incorrect Words | 15–50 s | click the transcript words that differ from what is said | Listening, Reading | 1 point per correct word, 1 point deducted per wrong click |
| 8 | Write from Dictation | 3–5 s | type the sentence you hear | Listening, Writing | 1 point per correct word |

\* The Listening page describes Multiple Choice, Multiple Answers only as partial credit. The Score Guide also deducts a point for each wrong choice, with a minimum of zero, and the app follows the Score Guide.
