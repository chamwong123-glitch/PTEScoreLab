# PTE Academic — Timing & Format Reference

An independent summary, not affiliated with Pearson, compiled from Pearson's official test-format pages (pearsonpte.com/pte-academic/test-format/), current as of September 2026. This is a companion to the official Score Guide — use the Score Guide for *how points are awarded*, and this doc for *exactly what happens on screen and how much time you get*.

---

## Part 1: Speaking & Writing (76–84 minutes, 9 question types)

### Personal Introduction *(unscored — familiarization only)*
- **Prep:** 25 seconds | **Speak:** 30 seconds
- Does not contribute to your score.

### 1. Read Aloud
- **Prompt:** Text up to 60 words
- **Prep/read time:** 30–40 seconds (varies by text length) | **Speak:** varies by text length
- **Skills:** Speaking
- Content, Pronunciation, Oral Fluency.

### 2. Repeat Sentence
- **Prompt:** Audio, 3–9 seconds
- **Speak:** 15 seconds (mic opens immediately after audio ends)
- **Skills:** Listening + Speaking
- Content, Pronunciation, Oral Fluency.

### 3. Describe Image
- **Prep:** 25 seconds | **Speak:** 40 seconds
- **Skills:** Speaking
- Content, Pronunciation, Oral Fluency. Human reviews Content.

### 4. Retell Lecture
- **Prompt:** Audio/video up to 90 seconds
- **Prep:** 10 seconds | **Speak:** 40 seconds
- **Skills:** Listening + Speaking
- Content, Pronunciation, Oral Fluency. Human reviews Content.

### 5. Answer Short Question
- **Prompt:** Audio, 3–9 seconds
- **Speak:** 10 seconds (no tone — mic opens immediately; if you wait >3 sec it's marked complete)
- **Skills:** Listening only
- Correct/incorrect only.

### 6. Summarize Group Discussion *(added Aug 2025)*
- **Prompt:** Audio, up to 3 minutes (3 speakers)
- **Prep:** 10 seconds | **Speak:** 2 minutes
- **Skills:** Listening + Speaking
- Content, Pronunciation, Oral Fluency. Human reviews Content.

### 7. Respond to a Situation *(added Aug 2025)*
- **Prompt:** Text/audio up to 60 words
- **Prep:** 10 seconds | **Speak:** 40 seconds
- **Skills:** Speaking only
- Content, Pronunciation, Oral Fluency. Human reviews Content.

### 8. Summarize Written Text
- **Prompt:** Text up to 300 words
- **Time:** 10 minutes total
- **Response:** ONE sentence, 5–75 words
- **Skills:** Reading + Writing
- Content, Form, Grammar, Vocabulary.

### 9. Write Essay
- **Prompt:** 2–3 sentence topic
- **Time:** 20 minutes total
- **Response:** 200–300 words
- **Skills:** Writing only
- Content, Development/Structure/Coherence, Form, General Linguistic Range, Grammar, Vocabulary, Spelling. Human reviews Content, DSC, GLR.

## Part 2: Reading (23–30 minutes, 5 question types)

All untimed per-item (section is timed overall).

| Question Type | Prompt Length | Skills |
|---|---|---|
| Fill in the Blanks (Dropdown) | Text up to 300 words | Reading |
| Multiple Choice, Multiple Answers | Text up to 350 words | Reading |
| Reorder Paragraph | Text up to 150 words | Reading |
| Fill in the Blanks (Drag and Drop) | Text up to 80 words | Reading (also touches Writing per Pearson's part intro; the Score Guide scores it for Reading only) |
| Multiple Choice, Single Answer | Text up to 300 words | Reading |

## Part 3: Listening (31–39 minutes, 8 question types)

All audio plays **once only, automatically**. Note-taking allowed throughout.

| Question Type | Prompt Length | Time to Answer | Skills |
|---|---|---|---|
| Summarize Spoken Text | 60–90 sec audio | 10 min to write (50–70 words) | Listening + Writing |
| Multiple Choice, Multiple Answers | 80–120 sec audio | Untimed | Listening |
| Fill in the Blanks (Type In) | 30–60 sec audio | Untimed | Listening |
| Highlight Correct Summary | 30–90 sec audio | Untimed | Listening + Reading |
| Multiple Choice, Single Answer | 30–90 sec audio | Untimed | Listening |
| Select Missing Word | 20–70 sec audio | Untimed | Listening |
| Highlight Incorrect Words | 15–50 sec audio | Untimed | Listening + Reading |
| Write from Dictation | 3–5 sec audio | Untimed | Listening + Writing |

---

## Universal rules across all Speaking tasks
- You can only record **once** — no retry, no replaying audio.
- If you stay silent for **more than 3 seconds** after the mic opens (or after starting, then pausing), the recording auto-stops and moves to "Completed" — you lose the rest of your chance to speak.
- A short tone signals when to start (except Answer Short Question, which has no tone).
